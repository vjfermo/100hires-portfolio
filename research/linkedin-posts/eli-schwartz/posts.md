## POST 1

**Date:January 2026
**Post:I just presented this with Google's CapitalG

The playbook that shows how to win search when AI owns the top of the funnel.

Most teams are still optimizing for 2021. They're chasing keywords. Building content farms. Praying the algorithm notices them.

Meanwhile, AI is eating the awareness stage alive.

Stop treating SEO as a content problem. It's a product problem.

The teams winning right now aren't writing more blog posts with prompts shoved into the content. They're building utility directly into search journeys.

Think templates. Tools. Category pages that solve the query by using your product, not just talking about it.

The shift is simple but brutal:

Old SEO: Get found, then convince them to try your product.

New AEO + SEO: Your product IS how they get found.

Four pillars that matter:

1. Deep customer understanding before keyword + prompt research

2. Focus on transactional intent (AI has robbed TOFU)

3. Build SEO products, not SEO content

4. Engineer AI citations through strategic relationship building and agencies

The companies that get this are winning their categories even with diminished search traffic.

The ones that don't are watching their organic traffic disappear and complaining. 

This shift is happening whether you're ready or not.

## POST 2

**Date:June 07, 2026
**Post:AEO was supposed to replace SEO.
That premise only worked if Google lost.

The whole narrative was built on ChatGPT and others eating into Google's dominance. 

Investors poured money into new AEO tools that were going to own this new space. (In diligence calls, many of the VCs didn't even know that there were already SEO tools!)

Consultants rebranded overnight to GEO/AEO/LLMEO. 

Everyone assumed the search landscape was about to flip to something brand new. 

But Google didn't lose, and now that's becoming obvious.

Google has structural advantages that were always going to be hard to beat:
- The index
- Deep financial pockets
- Default search on billions of devices
- User trust built over two decades
- Native hardware
- Data, data and more data


If Google stays dominant (and it looks like it will), AEO doesn't replace SEO. It just becomes a layer on top of it, with Google deciding how much of any given result is AI-generated vs. traditional.

The toolset for this world already exists whether it's GSC, Similarweb, Conductor, Ahrefs, Semrush, seoClarity (message me if I missed any large ones)

The only thing genuinely missing is an AI visibility feature inside tools like GSC which is coming.

So what happens to all the new AEO platforms built on the assumption that Google would lose? Most will struggle to justify their existence when the incumbent toolset just needs one new feature to cover the gap.

I am curious to hear what you think.

**Post:Everyone's saying DuckDuckGo is finally winning. The data doesn't back that up.
I pulled the Similarweb data to check. Here's what I found.

📉 Web traffic to duckduckgo.com has been in overall decline for 37 months. 
📉 Engagement metrics are down over the same period. There's a slight uptick in the last 28 days, but nothing dramatic. (see chart)

↗️If you read the media articles, the stat they're actually citing is a 30% spike in app installs during the week of Google I/O. That's real. But ....app installs aren't search usage according to this data. 

In the Similarweb data, the number that stood out most: 

👀 85% of DuckDuckGo visitors also use Google, and you can see how much bigger Google is than DuckDuckGo in the chart below. 

These aren't converts to DuckDuckGo. They are just curious dabblers who haven't switched anything.

Also, the noai.duckduckgo angle is smart PR because its tiny traffic leads to huge media coverage. 

Based on the data, the audience skews very young, which usually means high curiosity and low retention.

DuckDuckGo holds around 2% of the US search market, and a decade ago, when I ran a survey on search market share, they had 1%. 
After all this time and tens of millions spent on marketing, they haven't really moved the needle. 

To be fair, a 30% install spike off that base is a good week and outcome, but it is most definitely not a structural shift.

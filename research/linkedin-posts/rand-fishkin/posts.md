## POST 2

**Date:May 2026
**Post:The latest Datos, A Semrush Company State of Search dropped two weeks ago, and if you haven't spent some time in there, you might be falling for narratives that data (on 10s of millions of devices!) doesn't support.

Just a sample:
1) AI is disrupting traditional search! Nope. Look at the numbers.
2) OK... but AI is growing faster than search! Nope. On an absolute basis, traditional search is outpacing AI tool growth.
3) Fine, but AI Mode in Google is huge! Nope. It's itsy bitsy. Growing, but still less than 0.2% share.
4) ChatGPT is winning, but Claude is close behind! Nope. Claude's catching up, but Gemini is still in the #2 spot and growing, and ChatGPT has plateaued/declined for the past 6 months (Sept. 2025 was their high point)

Do yourself a favor -- download the dag nab report (with dozens more graphs and data points) and don't fall for unsupported narratives!

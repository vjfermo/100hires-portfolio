## POST 1

**Date:June 05, 2026
**Post:Big news: TOFU content is back!

If you search for product-led, truly top funnel topics, you will find company recommendations in AI results. 

The results come from the companies that have created this supporting top funnel content and are ranking for them, such as HubSpot, Salesforce, and Pipedrive in this example.

Previously, I was of the mind that if AI was simply summarizing content like this, there was little value doing it if clicks had dropped preciptiously. 

But if it holds up that AI will also recommend us on the right, product-connected content, it opens up a new universe of content, and in a lot of ways re-opens the creation universe for marketers.

The stress test our team is using: if it’s possible to achieve at least 50% brand visibility on these prompts, it’s a top funnel topic worth going after if the TOFU has significant search volume as a proxy of demand. 

As an example for HubSpot, I tested this “what is CRM” prompt for them and they achieved 83% visibility from a mention point of view. That’s not a top funnel topic wiping away our search universe – that’s exactly the kind of influence we want to have from content marketing.

I could see re-indexing our search and topic universe as a pulse check to determine which keywords are actually returning brands. High visibility returns above 50% could indicate value beyond the click worth investing in.

Kudos to Aja Frost for illuminating this reality, and to her team for their strong visibility in LLMs!

## POST 2

**Date:June 04, 2026
**Post:“AI attribution is solved with the new Search Console features”.

Not so fast. 

Google is adding impression data for *URLs* that appear in AI features. They are not reporting on the unlinked mentions in AI.

It is these mentions – at least until they become linked – that are the most valuable currently, and what we all need data on to better show the value of GEO programs.

Yes, this is a positive addition for search practitioners. But our problems are not solved.

In the example screenshot, as I understand from what’s being rolled out, Ahrefs and Semrush would not receive a tracked impression. Yet it is those two words that are receiving all the value from this prompt.

That’s problematic, because that’s the most important datapoint we are still all after and would love to see for the companies we help.

## POST 3

**Date:June 02, 2026
**Post:Our new B2B GEO study significantly changed how we prioritize content.

We tested every comparison content template against AI search traffic across 116 B2B sites. One type came back double the next-best option.

To our surprise, it wasn't "best X software" or “X alternatives" pages, it was actually the versus content format (X vs Y) that performed best.

We pulled GA4 data on 1,112 transactional pages and correlated each content type against AI search referrals from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, and Meta AI over 90 days.

Versus pages had a Spearman correlation of 0.65, or roughly 2x the next-best content type.

The reason makes structural sense in retrospect. When an LLM answers a comparison query, it cites pages that match the "X vs Y" intent. Best of lists and alternatives pages compete with G2, Capterra, and affiliate roundups for the same citation slot, and often return the homepage (or no page) instead. 

Direct comparisons have less competition and are a tighter intent match, and are therefore more likely to drive the ultimate click.

A few more findings from the study worth pulling out:

The first 20 transactional comparison pages created are where the traffic curve bends most. Going from one to five pages to six to 20 corresponds to a 350% median lift in AI search sessions. Going from there to 21+ adds another 250%. 

Second, content explains about 28% of the variance in B2B AI search traffic. The other 72% is brand pull and category fit. One in five of the top-traffic sites in our dataset had fewer than five transactional pages. 

How they got there? Brand.

So yes, it’s possible to get referenced without transactional comparison content. But if you aren’t a category leading brand (yet), that content may help you enter the conversation. 

Finally, an interesting byproduct here is that while it’s feasible that X vs Y content is best at driving visits, it’s possible “best X” and “X alternative” are still better at driving LLM *influence*, or at least more capable than the 2x gap may communicate.

It also makes me question how we build prompts: should we add another layer of BRAND vs BRAND comparison, making sure to assess sentiment at that level to proactively curate and solve for the gaps? It may make sense, given the clear leverage these posts create.

This role is going to be BIG and here's why

AI is great and there's so much we can do with it.

The marketing engineer sits between all marketing units and builds AI workflows, tools and automations that help teams improve efficiency, ensure quality control and gain better visibility into performance.

Ensures a single source of truth that becomes an AI lab.

## POST 3

**Date:April 2026
**Post:Can you use AI to write truly outstanding content?


The answer is yes, but there's a caveat

On their own, LLMs generate word salad that's never good enough for publication.

But in the hands of a gifted writer who knows how to bend words to their will, AI is magic!

Go to the Moz blog and read the op-eds, research work, and educational pieces I've published.

All written with the help of AI, right down to this post that went live this week

AI cannot make you a better writer. It's a machine that swallowed the internet and vomits out garbled words.

But if you understand storytelling

If you know where it hurts for your audience

If you have a strong voice and style...

You can personalize the output to reflect YOU

The problem is you're expecting too much from these LLMs that are literally machines!

Instead, take time to train it until its responses closely match your writing style:

Provide examples of past work every single time
Provide context on what you're trying to achieve...the more the better
Tell it what worked with the examples
Provide a style guide or writing instructions
Write in sections to maintain quality
Improve what it generates and share your revised copy with it
Tell it what mistakes it's making and how you fixed it
Rinse and repeat until it gets it right

And even then, always add your stank on the final copy so it looks like you, not something a machine wrote.

Stop believing the lies that LLMs are supposed to make you write faster.

Faster is relative when you care about quality.
